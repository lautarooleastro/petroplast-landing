## Success Metrics

### Technical Metrics
- **Page Load Speed**: < 3 seconds
- **Lighthouse Score**: > 90
- **Mobile Usability**: 100%
- **SEO Score**: > 90

### Business Metrics
- **Contact Form Submissions**: Track conversion rate
- **Page Views**: Monitor traffic growth
- **Bounce Rate**: < 50%
- **Time on Site**: > 2 minutes

## Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks
- **Browser Compatibility**: Test early and often
- **Mobile Responsiveness**: Mobile-first approach
- **Performance Issues**: Regular optimization
- **Form Functionality**: Thorough testing

### Content Risks
- **Missing Content**: Gather all content upfront
- **Image Quality**: Professional photography
- **Legal Compliance**: Privacy policy, terms
- **Accessibility**: WCAG guidelines compliance
